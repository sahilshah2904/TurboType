TurboType privacy policy

Guest play

TurboType stores guest progress and settings in this browser. Progress includes campaign clears, rewards, race summaries and typing statistics such as per-key counts and timing. You can export a backup from Progress.

Google sign-in and cloud saves

Sign-in is optional. Google shares your basic profile and email with Supabase, which manages authentication for TurboType. The game uses your profile name in the header. An approved email can save game progress to its own Supabase record and restore it in another browser.

TurboType stores gameplay summaries and aggregate typing statistics. It does not collect Google passwords or send raw keystrokes to the save service. GitHub Pages hosts the game and may log your IP address for security.

Export or delete your data

In Progress, open Save and backup tools to export your progress or delete your cloud save. Cloud save deletion removes gameplay progress. Contact the maintainer to request removal of the separate sign-in identity. Signing out clears the local account cache for that tab. Sync or export account progress before closing it.

Contact the TurboType maintainer on GitHub for questions. Keep personal account details out of public issues.
